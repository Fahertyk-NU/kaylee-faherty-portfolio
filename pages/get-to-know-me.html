How did you end up in data science?

Through my experience in chemical engineering, I completed a co-op in manufacturing and spent two years in R&D, both in the paper and packaging industry. I found that the work I enjoyed most was using data to tell a story and inform decision-making.

Querying Visualization Data Storytelling

I enrolled in Northeastern's Roux Institute Master's program to make that shift deliberate and structured. The engineering foundation turned out to transfer very well: an analytical problem-solving approach, a strong technical background, and experience communicating across many different business functions.

What do you do outside of work and school?

I love to be outside which in Maine means skiing, snowshoeing and ice skating in the winter and golfing, hiking, and camping in the summer.

Outside of being outdoors, I also love to cook and bake, and recently started making my own bread. I also enjoy knitting, working-out, and always have a podcast going.

Favorite genre of music?

Country is definitely my favorite genre, but my playlists are pretty varied. You'll also find pop, hip-hop, indie, and occasionally some rock depending on the mood.

Lake or Ocean?

Growing up in Maine I was lucky enough to be able to spend a lot of time on the water, and I really love both. But, if I had to choose, I'd say ocean.

Favorite thing about living in Maine?

The close proximity to nature and the abundance of outdoor activities. Every season has something special to offer - from the ocean and lakes to the mountains, there's always something to explore and enjoy.

Coffee or Tea?

Definitely tea - I actually don't like coffee at all. Green tea is my go-to, hot in the winter and iced in the summer.
